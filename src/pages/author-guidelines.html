  5. Acceptance & publicationAccepted articles are prepared in the journal format and published open access in a forthcoming issue.

Preparing Your Manuscript

File format

  • Submit the manuscript as a Microsoft Word, OpenOffice or RTF file.
  • Text is single-spaced, in a 12-point font. Use italics rather than underlining (except for URLs).
  • Place illustrations, figures and tables within the text at the appropriate points, not at the end.

Structure

  • Title of the article.
  • Author details: name, designation, department, institution, country and email address of every author.
  • Abstract, followed by keywords.
  • Main text with clear section headings, for example Introduction, Discussion and Conclusion.
  • References at the end. Provide URLs for references where available.

Research Integrity

Originality

The submission must not have been published before, nor be under consideration by another journal.

Plagiarism certificate

Submit a plagiarism-check certificate with your article. Every article is checked again with iThenticate, including for AI-generated content, before acceptance.

Blind peer review

Remove your name and affiliation from the file properties and the review copy, so reviewers cannot identify you.
