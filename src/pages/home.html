About the Journal

eISSN: 2583-6137 | Frequency: Bi-annual | Since: 2022

The Journal of Cultural Research Studies (JCRS) is a blind peer-reviewed, open access, multidisciplinary and multilingual journal published by the Development and Research Academy for Folk and Tribes (DRAFT), Kolkata. It publishes research papers, book reviews, theses and video documentation on folklore, tribal and indigenous studies, literature, language, performing and visual arts, regional heritage and allied fields of the Social Sciences and Humanities, in English and Bengali.
